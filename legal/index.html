Terms

  • Playing on CnC means following the Rules and the Cfx.re terms of service.
  • We may change, reset or remove parts of the game (money, items, cars, jobs) to fix bugs, balance the game or keep it running.
  • We may suspend or ban accounts that break the rules. A ban can include store perks; see Refunds.

Store purchases

  • Payments are taken by Tebex, who handle billing, receipts, tax and refunds. You log in with your Cfx.re account at checkout; that account receives the purchase.
  • Purchases are perks and in-game credit. They are a licence to use them on CnC, not property, and have no cash value.
  • VIP bank money is in-game money for one character, claimed in game. It has no cash value and may be taken back from that character if the payment for it is refunded or charged back.
  • CnCoins are credit for the store in game (F5). They have no cash value, cannot be traded, sold or moved to another account, and coins already spent are not refunded. They may be taken back if the payment for them is refunded or charged back.
  • VIP memberships renew every month until you cancel them from your Tebex payment history. Perks stay until the end of the paid month.
  • Purchases arrive on the server within minutes and are claimed in game with F5 → My purchases.
  • Perks may change over time as the game changes; we'll keep their value equal or better.

Refunds

  • Something wrong or missing? Write to us before you claim it and we'll fix it or refund it.
  • Once a purchase is claimed in game it is considered delivered and is final, except where the law says otherwise.
  • A chargeback or refund removes the purchase in game, and can lead to a ban from the store.
  • Accounts banned for cheating, exploiting or real-money trading lose their perks without refund.

Privacy

  • This website has no accounts, no ads and no tracking cookies. It remembers your cart and your release-notes language in your own browser only.
  • The game server stores what it needs to run: your FiveM and Rockstar identifiers, your characters and progress, chat and reports sent to staff, and anti-cheat logs.
  • For the store we store your Cfx.re id, what you bought and when, and what you claimed. Tebex handles your payment details; we never see them.
  • We don't sell or share your data. Ask us to see or delete your data at the address below; anti-cheat and payment records may be kept as long as the law or fraud prevention needs.
