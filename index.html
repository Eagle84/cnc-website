What's in the city

More than a chase.

Heat & wanted stars

Seen crimes earn stars. Four or more and you're on Breaking News with a price on your head.

Five gangs

Families, Ballas, Vagos, Marabunta, the Lost. Earn Family status for backup and a gang car.

The Hacker

Ten hard levels. Black hat skims ATMs and blinds cameras; white hat patches the city for clean pay.

The Dark Web

Anonymous contracts on other players. Finish them for skill points: suppressors, grapple lines and more.

Heists

The Union Depository, the armored truck, the prison wall. Get the kit, bring the crew, beat the clock.

Syndicate duos

Link up with a friend: shared stash, shared skill tree, shared heat, and heists built for two.

Off the grid

Dive wrecks at the marina, hunt the swamp, race the Mud Club — or smuggle a crate past the coast guard.

Modes

Deathmatch, Gun Game and Street Races when you just want to fight or drive.
