Rules

Play hard. Play fair.

CnC is a chase, not a fight club. These rules keep it fun for the side that's running and the side that's chasing. Staff have the final word.

General Combat & chases Police & EMS Criminals & gangs Chat & voice Cheats & exploits Punishments Appeals
Short version: respect people, don't kill without a reason, don't cheat, and don't use the game's bugs. When in doubt, ask staff on Discord or with F4 → Help.

General

  1. Respect everyone. No harassment, hate speech, threats or discrimination — in game, in chat, on voice or on Discord.
  2. One account, one person. Don't share accounts or play on someone else's.
  3. Your job is your side. Crime is for civilians, the Drug Dealer and the underworld careers. Police, medics and other jobs don't do crime — the game greys it out, and working around that is an exploit.
  4. Don't log out to escape. Leaving while cuffed, wanted, in a fight or during a robbery to dodge the outcome (combat logging) is not allowed.
  5. Staff decisions stand during the session. If you disagree, follow the call now and appeal later.

Combat & chases

  1. No random killing. Shooting someone needs a reason the game gives you: a robbery, a chase, a gang war, a Dark Web contract, a bounty, or someone attacking you first.
  2. No vehicle ramming for fun. Using a car as a weapon outside a chase or a fight you're part of is not allowed.
  3. Hands up means hands up. When someone surrenders (X) and they're not a threat, cuff them, rob them or let them go — don't execute them.
  4. Leave the hospital, prison and spawn alone. No camping, baiting or attacking at Pillbox, Bolingbroke or where players appear.
  5. Modes are for fighting. Deathmatch, Gun Game and Street Races (/modes) are the place for fights without a story.

Police & EMS

  1. Serve, don't hunt. Respond to calls, stop crimes and make arrests. Don't harass players who aren't wanted.
  2. Give a chance to surrender at 1–2 stars when it's safe. Lethal force is for armed suspects and 3+ stars.
  3. No corruption that breaks the game. Taking a roleplay bribe is part of the city; handing your gear, cars or information to criminals is not.
  4. Medics stay neutral. Treat anyone who's down, cop or criminal, and don't take part in the fight.
  5. Use police tools for police work. Cameras, trackers, the MDT and the radar are for active cases only.

Criminals & gangs

  1. Crimes need police on duty where the game says so (banks and heists). Stores, ATMs and houses can be robbed any time — the NPC police still come.
  2. Hostages are part of the robbery, not a way to hold one player for a long time. Let them go when the job ends.
  3. Gang wars stay on turf. Fight over turfs and contracts the game gives you; don't start a war with a player who isn't part of it.
  4. Dark Web contracts are on the server's terms: the board picks the mark, the server sees the hit. No setting up a friend to farm contracts.
  5. Crews of up to four for a heist, unless the job says otherwise.

Chat & voice

  1. Keep it readable. No spam, walls of caps or flooding.
  2. No advertising other servers, stores or services.
  3. The Dark Web and Ads tabs are for in-game business, not real-world deals.
  4. No sharing personal information — yours or anyone else's.
  5. Voice: no ear-rape, soundboards or music over open mic.

Cheats & exploits

  1. No cheats, mod menus, injectors or macros. The anti-cheat logs everything and bans are permanent.
  2. No exploiting bugs for money, items, XP or to escape the police. Found a bug? Report it with F4 → Help → Report a bug — good reports get rewarded.
  3. No real-money trading of in-game money, items, cars or accounts. It's against the Cfx.re rules and gets both sides banned.

Punishments

WhatUsually
First small break (spam, a careless ram)A warning
Repeated or deliberate breaksKick, then a temporary ban (1–7 days)
Random killing sprees, combat logging, harassmentTemporary ban (3–30 days)
Cheating, exploiting for profit, real-money trading, hate speechPermanent ban

Money, items or cars gained by breaking the rules are taken back. Staff can step outside this table when a case needs it.

Appeals

Think a decision was wrong? Open a ticket on our Discord within 14 days with your Cfx.re name, what happened and when. Clips help. Be honest — appeals that admit the mistake are usually shortened.
